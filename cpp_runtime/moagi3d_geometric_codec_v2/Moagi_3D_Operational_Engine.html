ALGEBRAIC COMPUTATIONAL CONTRACT

Executable implementation

Geometry: r=(x,y,z) ∈ [-1,1]³; X(r)=round(250 exp(-d(r)² / 2σ²) + noise).

Encode: z*=(BᵀB + λI)⁻¹BᵀX, with 3D Gaussian dictionary B ∈ ℝ⁷⁸⁴ˣ¹⁶.

Inward iteration: zₜ₊₁=ρzₜ+(1−ρ)z*, 0≤ρ<1. The exact contraction is ||zₜ−z*||₂=ρᵗ||z₀−z*||₂ up to floating-point error.

Decode: X̂=clip(round(B z),0,255). Exact residual: ε=X XOR X̂, X'=ε XOR X̂=X.

Accounting: 16 Q10 int32 latents = 64 B; the exact file also carries a residual and a 16 B header. The actual byte ratio is measured, not inferred from 784/16.
