# Code of Conduct

Jarvis-X is a technical research project. Participation requires respectful, evidence-oriented collaboration.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people;
- distinguish observations, assumptions and conclusions;
- give specific, reproducible technical feedback;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes openly;
- respect different backgrounds, experience levels and communication styles;
- protect private, medical, security-sensitive and personally identifying information;
- credit prior work and contributors accurately;
- accept maintainers' responsibility to constrain scope and claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, discrimination or targeted humiliation;
- sexualized attention or imagery;
- publishing another person's private information;
- deliberately misleading performance, safety or intelligence claims;
- submitting malicious code, hidden telemetry, credential theft or destructive payloads;
- sustained disruption of technical discussion;
- impersonation or false attribution;
- retaliation against good-faith vulnerability reports or critical review.

## Technical disagreement

Strong technical disagreement is welcome when it remains focused on requirements, evidence, tests, benchmarks, threat models and trade-offs.

Critique the implementation or claim, not the person's intelligence, profession, identity or motives.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject or remove contributions and interactions that violate this policy. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Security-sensitive incidents should be reported according to `SECURITY.md`. Other conduct concerns may be raised in a minimal issue requesting a private discussion channel when no private channel is available.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, commits and project-related public interactions where a participant represents the project.

## Attribution

This policy is project-specific and informed by widely used open-source community standards. It may evolve as the contributor community grows.
