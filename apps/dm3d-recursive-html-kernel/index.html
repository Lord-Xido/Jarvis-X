Process up to one million generated HTML source lines or 64-byte windows of the active multimodal source. Every counted cycle executes both reversible transforms and verifies every original byte. Work is chunked to keep the page responsive; ANN training is a separate, measured approximation.

Engineering boundaries. The logical address space is (230)3 = 290 locations, not 290 bytes of allocated RAM. The 3D view is a sampled projection of at most 4,096 source bytes, not full-volume storage. Lossless guarantee applies to a valid .dmxm archive via exact byte comparison on locally produced archives (and checksums on imported archives); CRC-32 and SHA-256 are integrity aids, not authenticated encryption.
