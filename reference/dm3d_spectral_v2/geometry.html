Convergence and verification
Fourier modes are divided by their exact periodic-lattice eigenvalues, then decoded into physical 3D coordinates. The new state is accepted only when the actual discretised objective is finite and no greater than the previous objective.

Why the direct path wins

The iterative solver repeatedly computes 3D stencil updates; the FFT solver diagonalises the same operator and solves it once. This speedup does not extend automatically to arbitrary nonlinear neural models.
